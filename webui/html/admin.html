    Users

    Create and manage accounts. A Listener can play and download music; an Uploader can also add files; an Admin can do everything. What a listener may actually reach is still governed by access groups and guest-playable flags below.

    Access groups

    Groups bundle users and grant them access to content scopes (whole library, an artist, an album, or a single file). A listener can only play what a group they belong to is granted — plus anything marked guest-playable.
