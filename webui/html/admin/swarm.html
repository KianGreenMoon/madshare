Rate limits

These cap what this node's swarm traffic costs your line. They apply to everyone, friends included — they describe your pipe, not who deserves what. Leave a field empty to use the value from madshare.toml; enter 0 for unlimited.

How big should these be?

Size them against your uplink, not the download speed your ISP quotes: KiB/s ≈ Mbit/s × 128. Leaving about a quarter of the uplink free is a good starting point — so a 20 Mbit uplink (≈2560 KiB/s) suggests roughly 1900.

Do not set them small. One listener streaming CD-rate FLAC needs ~125 KiB/s continuously, and every concurrent transfer shares the same bucket. Below that, this node does not merely get slower: fetching peers time it out, the swarm de-ranks it and goes elsewhere, and the bytes you already sent are wasted. If your line cannot spare ~256 KiB/s, switching seeding off on Settings is the honest setting rather than a starvation rate.

The download cap also slows anything you are listening to from the network, since the player reads from the same transfer.
