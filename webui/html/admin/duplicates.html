Duplicates & variants

Recordings that hold more than one rendition of the same audio (matched by acoustic fingerprint). The best rendition by the quality ladder is marked. Absorb into ★ keeps one rendition's audio (the ★ master by default — pick another with the Keep radio) and drops the others' files while preserving every distinct release as its own track; duplicate or blank releases are removed. Absorb all → keep best does this for every recording at once. Split off detaches a rendition when the fingerprint grouped two genuinely different versions, and Delete sends a rendition to Trash.
