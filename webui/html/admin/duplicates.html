Duplicates & variants

Recordings that hold more than one rendition of the same audio (matched by acoustic fingerprint). The best rendition by the quality ladder is marked. Tick the redundant renditions, then Absorb selected folds them into the best unticked rendition of each recording — dropping their files (restorable) while preserving every distinct release as its own track; duplicate or blank releases are removed. Select all non-best ticks exactly the redundant copies, so it + Absorb keeps each recording's best across the page. Split off detaches a rendition when the fingerprint grouped two genuinely different versions, and Delete selected sends renditions to Trash.
