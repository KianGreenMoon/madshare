Recordings

Every recording — the same-audio grouping behind the library. A recording holds renditions (the audio files, ranked by the quality ladder) and appearances (the tagsets listeners see as tracks). Expand a recording to curate both arms; tick two or more and Merge folds them into one. Dormant recordings (no playable rendition) are hidden from the library — this page is where you find and revive them.

Tick recordings to merge or trash them.

Merge recordings

The other recordings fold into the target: renditions union and re-rank on the quality ladder, identical appearances collapse (the target’s copy wins), the target keeps its primary. Reversible only piecewise (Split off / Move) — merge has no single undo.

Move appearance

Type to search for the target recording.

The appearance will play the target recording’s best rendition. An identical appearance already there refuses the move.

License & guest access

Recording-level: applies to every appearance and every rendition at once. An explicit guest setting is a manual decision the license auto-derive policy never overrides.
