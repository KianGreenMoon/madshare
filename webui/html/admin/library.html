Library

Every recording — the same-audio grouping behind the library. A recording holds renditions (the audio files, ranked by the quality ladder) and appearances (the tagsets listeners see as tracks). Expand a recording to curate both arms; tick two or more and Merge folds them into one. Dormant recordings (no playable rendition) are hidden from the library — this lens is where you find and revive them.

Tick recordings to merge or trash them.

Merge recordings

The other recordings fold into the target: renditions union and re-rank on the quality ladder, identical appearances collapse (the target’s copy wins), the target keeps its primary. Reversible only piecewise (Split off / Move) — merge has no single undo.

Access & sharing

Recording-level: applies to every appearance and every rendition at once. An explicit guest setting is a manual decision the license auto-derive policy never overrides. Madnetwork scope decides how far this recording travels along the friendship chain — it is enforced on this node’s own catalog and byte serving; reach beyond direct friends arrives with friend-list gossip.
